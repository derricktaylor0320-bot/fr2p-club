⏰ How the 30-Day Commission Hold Works

The 30-day hold ensures members stay active before sponsors get paid. This protects against fraud and chargebacks while maintaining industry-standard payout timing.

(January)
Action: New member joins and pays $25 subscription
Commission: Sponsor earns $5 → HELD for 30 days
Status: Commission recorded but not paid yet

(February)
Action: Member's subscription renews automatically ($25)
Commission: January's $5 → RELEASED & PAID

Sponsor Receives: $5 (from January)

🎯 Why $10K-$25K is the Perfect Amount

The Commission Float Explained

What is a commission float?
It's the capital needed to pay commissions while waiting for subscriptions to renew. Think of it like a security deposit that stays in the business - it's not spent, just cycled through the payout system.
