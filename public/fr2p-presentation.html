📋 Commission Eligibility & Account Policies

⏱️ 60-Day Commission Eligibility Rule

"Separating the Curious from the Serious" — At FR2P, we believe in rewarding committed members. Before you can start earning commissions, you must be enrolled and paid for 2 consecutive months (60 days).

  • Month 1 = Building & Learning - Your first month is dedicated to learning the system, setting up your profile, and beginning to share your referral link
  • Month 2 Payment = Proves Commitment - When you make your second monthly payment, it shows you're serious about building your financial future
  • Commission Eligibility Unlocked - After 60 days of active paid membership, you begin earning $5/month for every active referral
  • Why This Matters - This policy ensures our commission pool goes to dedicated members who are truly invested in the FR2P movement
Think of it this way: Month 1 is your training period. Month 2 proves you're serious. From Day 61 onward, you're earning commissions on every active referral you've brought in. The 60-day rule protects the community and rewards commitment.

🔄 90-Day Account Grace Period

Life happens. If you need to take a break from FR2P, we've got you covered with our 90-day account grace period.

  • Account Stays Active for 90 Days - If you stop paying, your account remains in our system for 90 days
  • Pick Up Where You Left Off - During the grace period, log back in with your username and password and everything is still there — same referrals, same network, same progress
  • No Penalty for Returning - Simply reactivate your membership within 90 days and continue building your financial roadway
  • After 90 Days - If you don't return within 90 days, your account is removed and you would need to rejoin as a new member
Peace of Mind: We understand that financial situations change. The 90-day grace period gives you breathing room without losing your hard-earned network and progress. Your referrals and achievements are preserved while you take care of life.
